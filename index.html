§ 01

Sectors

Four divisions. One standard of proof, set by the first.

The Group is organized as four numbered divisions. The technology division is the headline sector and the working portfolio; the others are presented exactly as they stand — operating, developing, or in pursuit — with nothing stated ahead of the record.

01

AI Systems & Technology

Working portfolio

The Group’s technology division designs and builds intelligent systems end to end. This is not a services menu the firm hopes to sell one day — it is a working portfolio, published and hash-verified, and it is the standard of proof the other divisions are held to.

  • NEUROGEN / SPHERION — cognitive software architecture with measured, reproducible benchmark results
  • The Hope line — autonomous agent systems, built as a single self-contained file
  • Hope Pi — self-maintaining autonomous hardware nodes
  • Hope Bridge — device-integration software connecting systems across platforms
02

Property Management

Operating

The Group manages residential property with the same operating discipline it applies to software: documented condition, scheduled maintenance, straight accounting. Growth in this division is deliberate — properties are added when they can be managed to the standard, not before.

03

Finance & Ventures

In development

The Group is developing its finance and venture interests — evaluating positions and structures with a long-term, cash-disciplined approach. Nothing in this division is offered to the public, and nothing here is investment advice; it is the Group’s own capital, put to work under the same rule as everything else: verified, or it doesn’t ship.

04

Federal Contracting

In pursuit

The Group is pursuing registration and eligibility to compete for United States federal contracts, building on its technology portfolio. The capabilities being brought to that market are the ones already proven in the portfolio: intelligent systems, autonomous operation, and verification discipline.

Lead system · Cognitive architecture

NEUROGEN / SPHERION

Six engines. One verdict.

SPHERION is the current release of the NEUROGEN line: a cognitive software architecture built from six engines — concept, relationship, activation, competition, adaptive memory, and reasoning/execution — with Go principles implemented as computation, a language layer, and pipeline integration.

It ships as a hash-verified release package, tested against its frozen predecessors byte for byte. In its published four-configuration experiments, the full SPHERION configuration scored 1.000 against a 0.830 baseline — with an unsupported-inference rate of 0.000 and 100% reproducibility across runs. The experiments also record what did not work: activation for candidate selection did not ship. Both results are published. That is the point of the standard.
