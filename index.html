Lead system: cognitive architecture

NEUROGEN / SPHERION

Six engines. One verdict.

SPHERION is the current release of the NEUROGEN line: a cognitive software architecture built from six engines — concept, relationship, activation, competition, adaptive memory, and reasoning/execution — with Go principles implemented as computation, a language layer, and pipeline integration.

It ships as a hash-verified release package, tested against its frozen predecessors byte for byte. In its published four-configuration experiments, the full SPHERION configuration scored 1.000 against a 0.830 baseline — with an unsupported-inference rate of 0.000 and 100% reproducibility across runs. The experiments also record what did not work: activation for candidate selection did not ship. Both results are published. That is the point of the standard.
